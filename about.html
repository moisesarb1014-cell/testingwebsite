Our Story

Rooted in Goleta, built on honest work

Reyes Gardening Services is a family-owned landscaping company serving Santa Barbara County — one honest estimate and one well-kept yard at a time.

Meet the Owner

Gerson Reyes

Gerson founded Reyes Gardening Services on a simple standard: show up when you say you will, tell clients the truth about price and plant viability, and do work that still looks good years later.

That approach shows up directly in client feedback — reviewers consistently point to honesty about pricing, accurate scheduling, and quality that holds up. Gerson personally assesses every project, from a single irrigation repair to a full backyard renovation, and stays the point of contact through completion.

Today, Reyes Gardening Services is growing beyond individual residential projects into recurring commercial and HOA grounds contracts across Santa Barbara County — bringing that same hands-on standard to every property, no matter the size.
